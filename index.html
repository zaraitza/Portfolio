How I work

The best teams aren’t the ones that never fail. They’re the ones that adapt fastest. These three habits help teams move with clarity, even in chaos.

Coach, don’t direct

Teams write and refine their own stories against a shared Definition of Ready. My job is to help them own the work, not to do it for them.

Make work visible

Velocity, burndown, and quality in Jira dashboards, plus Lean thinking to spot waiting, rework, and blockers before they cost a sprint.

Keep teams aligned

Scrum of Scrums, clear tickets, and early dependency checks keep teams in different countries and time zones moving together.
